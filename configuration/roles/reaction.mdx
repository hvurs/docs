---
title: Reaction Roles
description: "Configure self-assignable roles through the admin panel."
icon: hand-sparkles
---

Open `/admin` and choose **Reaction Roles** to manage mappings. A mapping connects a reaction on a message to a role Caesar can assign.

To add a mapping, provide the message, emoji, and role when prompted. To remove one, choose the mapping from the panel. Members can then react to the configured message to receive the role.

For the mapping to work:

- Caesar needs permission to add reactions in the message channel.
- Caesar needs **Manage Roles**, and its highest role must be above the role it assigns.
- The selected message and role must still exist in the server.

If assignment fails, check those permissions and the role order before recreating the mapping.
