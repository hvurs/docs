---
title: Leveling
description: "Manage Caesar's leveling system from its administration panel."
icon: ranking-star
---

Run `/level admin` to open the leveling panel. Authorized staff can manage the leveling system and review its available settings there.

Members can view their progress using the level commands offered by Discord's slash-command picker. The server's level rankings are part of `/leaderboard`; choose the **Levels** board.

If a leveling setting is unavailable, check your staff access. Use `/help` for the current member-facing level commands.
