---
title: Command Access
description: "Understand why Caesar commands or panel sections may be unavailable."
icon: shield
---

Caesar restricts staff commands and panel sections to authorized members. Some Discord commands also require native Discord permissions such as **Manage Roles**.

An unavailable command may not be registered for your role, or you may lack the permission required to use it. A panel can also hide sections you are not allowed to view or edit.

Ask a server administrator to review your access. For role assignment problems, check the bot's role position and permissions in Discord.
