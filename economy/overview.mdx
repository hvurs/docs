## Staff panel

Run `/economy admin` to open the staff economy panel. Authorized staff can use it to inspect and manage member accounts and economy settings, including adjusting a member's balance and managing item or organisation records.

The panel is limited to members with the configured economy staff permission; server administrators bypass that gate. If you cannot open it, ask a server administrator to review your access.

For published base odds and risks, see [Economy Chances](/economy/chances).
