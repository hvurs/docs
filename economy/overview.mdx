## Player commands

Economy actions are available as individual slash commands, such as `/daily`, `/work`, `/bank`, `/fish`, and `/crime`. Type `/` and select Caesar to browse the current options, or use `/help`.

The economy includes earning, banking, jobs, items, crafting, expeditions, and games. Some actions have cooldowns or risks; read each command's response before confirming an action.

## Staff panel

Run `/economy admin` to open the staff economy panel. Authorized staff can use it to inspect and manage member accounts and economy settings, including adjusting a member's balance.

The admin panel is permission-restricted. If you cannot open it, ask a server administrator to review your access.

For published base odds and risks, see [Economy Chances](/economy/chances).
