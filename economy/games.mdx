All gambling games are grouped under `/games`. Discord shows each game's options after you choose it. Wagering bets must be within the configured minimum and maximum; amounts accept values such as `1k`, `2.5m`, `50%`, `half`, or `all`. A game's payout is not a guarantee of profit. See [Economy Chances](/economy/chances) for published odds.

### `/games bet`

Roll a 12-sided die against Caesar. The higher roll wins a random share of your bet; a tie returns the stake.

### `/games blackjack`

Play a hand against the dealer. Choose to hit, stand, or double down. Blackjack pays 3:2, and the dealer stands on 17.

### `/games coinflip`

Choose heads or tails and place a bet. A win pays even money; a small edge result goes to the house.

### `/games fight`

Challenge another member to a turn-based fight. They must accept. Punch is reliable, kick deals more damage but can miss, defend reduces incoming damage and heals, and flee forfeits your stake. You can wager money; other members can place side bets during the opening turns.

### `/games highlow`

Guess whether a hidden number is higher or lower than the hint. Matching the hint wins the jackpot. This game is free to play and has a cooldown.

### `/games horserace`

Choose one of five horses and place a bet. Favorites win more often with smaller payouts; longshots win less often but can pay much more.

### `/games house`

View the casino's recorded wagers and payouts by game, including its kept share and player totals.

### `/games roulette`

Place a bet on red, black, green, even, odd, low, high, a dozen, or a number from 0 to 36. Payouts depend on the selected bet.

### `/games scratch`

Buy a scratch ticket and choose one of five hidden cards. A card may contain coins, an item, or no prize. If you do not pick in time, the ticket is refunded.

### `/games slots`

Place a bet and spin the slot machine. The payout depends on the symbols shown.

### `/games smuggle`

Stake money on a contraband run. At each checkpoint, decide whether to risk the next leg for a larger multiplier or sell the load. Later checkpoints are more dangerous; getting caught loses the stake and may lead to arrest. This activity unlocks at a required level and has a cooldown.

### `/games snakeeyes`

Roll two dice. One 1 pays 2.5 times the bet; two 1s pay 10 times the bet. Other results lose the stake.
