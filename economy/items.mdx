### `/shop`

Browse items sold directly by the shop and their prices.

### `/buy`

Buy a shop item by name and optionally specify a quantity, for example `/buy item:shovel`. Up to 100 can be purchased at once. Usable items include a **Use now** button after purchase.

### `/inventory`

View your items, quantities, and shop sell values. Active item perks are shown too. Listing a perk item on the market pauses its perk while it is listed.

### `/item`

Browse item categories or request details about a specific item. Item details include where it comes from, its sell value, drop chances when known, your quantity, and market listings.

### `/use`

Choose a usable item from your inventory or use an item by name and quantity. Items can grant timed boosts, reset cooldowns, expand your bank, find treasure, or protect your wallet and bank. Check `/item` to read an item's effect before using it.

### `/craft`

Browse recipes or craft an item from its ingredients. You can craft multiple items when you have enough materials; ingredients are only consumed when the complete recipe is available.

### `/collection`

Check collection sets and your progress. Use the claim option to exchange a complete set for its reward. Claimed items are consumed, including any legendary items and their active perks. Some everyday collections can be claimed repeatedly.

### `/sell`

Sell items to the shop for fixed prices. You can sell a type or category in bulk; protected tools, usable items, collectibles, and legendary items must be sold individually. Bulk selling keeps one of each perk item.

### `/market`

Trade items with other members. Browse listings, choose `list` to create one, `buy` to purchase from a listing, `mine` to view your active listings, or `cancel` to recover an item. Listings expire after seven days. A seller tax applies when a listing sells.
