---
title: Server Booster Economy Perks
description: "Ongoing economy benefits and the one-time reward for server boosters."
icon: bolt
---

Members actively boosting the server, or holding the server's configured booster role, receive these ongoing economy perks:

| Perk | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible command earnings | 25% more on payouts that use the standard earnings multiplier. The `/profile` panel shows the multiplier sources that apply to you. |
| Bank capacity | An extra 50,000 bank capacity. |
| Loans | 1.5x your calculated borrowing limit and half the normal loan interest rate. |
| Transfers | 4% tax on `/pay` and `/gift`, instead of the standard 8%. Trusted members of the same organisation pay no transfer tax. |

These are separate from the one-time `/boosterreward`. Members who are actively boosting can claim $35,000 and a Vault Expansion once per account. The claim stays used if you stop boosting and later boost again; holding a booster role by itself does not qualify for the one-time reward.

Active boosters also receive an extra giveaway entry by default. See [Giveaways](/miscellaneous/giveaway) for details.
